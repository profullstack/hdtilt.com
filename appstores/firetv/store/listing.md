## Short description

An open-source IPTV player for your own playlist, with a TV guide.

## Long description

hdtilt is a free, open-source IPTV player for the M3U or Xtream Codes playlist you already have. It comes with no channels: add your own playlist at hdtilt.com and it follows you to your TV, phone, computer and terminal.

- Live TV with groups, favorites and a filter that finds any channel in a few key presses
- A full TV guide (XMLTV), with catch-up where your provider offers it
- Movies and shows from Xtream Codes lines, with subtitles
- Made for the remote: big focus highlight, page up and down buttons, Back walks through every screen
- Your playlists, favorites and recent channels sync across every device you sign in on
- Free and open source (MIT): github.com/profullstack/hdtilt.com

hdtilt does not provide any content. You need a playlist from a provider you are entitled to watch.
